Why three tickets

Etihad will not sell the Abu Dhabi stop on one ticket

On one ticket, Etihad's site refuses a stop in Abu Dhabi shorter than 96 hours. It shows "restricted connection within 96 hours". With separate tickets, there is no stop to refuse.

Before you go

Things to do

1

Book the three tickets

Use the three buttons. Put both of you on the one Krabi booking. On separate tickets, Etihad does not rebook a missed flight, but you have more than a day of buffer.

2

Book a hotel for Sunday night

One night, Sun Nov 29. Ask for a late checkout on Monday. The flight leaves at 8:55 PM. Not booked yet.

3

Sit together

You share one booking on EY 422, so choose two seats next to each other.

4

Bags

Will has 1 checked bag to Abu Dhabi. Yana's Moscow flight and the Krabi flight have no checked bags. Add bags if you need them.

5

Payment

If Yana's Russian card does not work on etihad.com, Will can pay with his card.

Entry to the UAE

Will gets a free visa when he lands. Yana needs no visa: Russian citizens can stay up to 90 days.

Etihad's free stopover hotel does not apply to this plan, because it needs a round-trip Etihad ticket.
