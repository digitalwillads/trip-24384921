Page 2Krabi airport to Ao Nang to Koh Phi Phi

Ao Nang first. Then Koh Phi Phi.

You land in Krabi and stay 5 nights at Aree Tara in Ao Nang, Krabi's main beach town, about 30 minutes from the airport. On Sun Dec 6, a boat from Ao Nang takes you to Koh Phi Phi in 1 to 2 hours, for 4 nights at SAii Phi Phi Island Village. Phi Phi has no airport, so the boat is how you get there.

The first three leave from Nopparat Thara pier and land at Tonsai Pier, where you take a longtail to SAii. December is high season, so more boats run. Book a day or two ahead.

Good to know

Before the boat

Bring cash

The ฿20 island fee and the longtail fares are paid in cash.

Maya Bay

Day tours from Phi Phi go to Maya Bay. The marine park fee is ฿400 each.

Book ahead

December is high season. Book the boat a day or two before.

Money

$1 is about ฿34.5. Prices here are in Thai baht (฿).
