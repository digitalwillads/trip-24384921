Page 2Krabi airport to Ao Nang to Koh Phi Phi

Ao Nang first. Then Koh Phi Phi.

You land in Krabi and stay in Ao Nang, Krabi's main beach town, about 30 minutes from the airport. For your island days, a boat from Ao Nang takes you to Koh Phi Phi in 1 to 2 hours. Phi Phi has no airport, so the boat is how you get there.

About 2 hFerry from Ao Nang to Phi Phi (50 min by speedboat)
About 30 minTaxi from Krabi airport to Ao Nang

All three leave from Nopparat Thara pier and land at Tonsai Pier. December is high season, so more boats run. Book a day or two ahead.

Where you stay on Phi Phi

Tonsai or Long Beach

Tonsai village

Next to the pier, so you walk to your hotel. It is the busy center of the island. Phi Phi Island Cabana is here, about 250 m from the pier.

Walk from the pierBusy

Long Beach

Away from the village and quieter. A longtail boat from Tonsai Pier takes 5 to 10 minutes. Phi Phi Long Beach Resort & Villa is here.

Longtail ฿100–150 eachQuiet

Good to know

Before the boat

Bring cash

The ฿20 island fee and the longtail fares are paid in cash.

Maya Bay

Day tours from Phi Phi go to Maya Bay. The marine park fee is ฿400 each.

Book ahead

December is high season. Book the boat a day or two before.

Money

$1 is about ฿34.5. Prices here are in Thai baht (฿).
